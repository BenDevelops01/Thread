THREAD BETA 0.7.0 — Apple silicon, macOS 14+

Drag Thread Beta.app into Applications, then open it there.

This early developer beta is ad-hoc signed, NOT notarized by Apple.
If macOS blocks the first launch, dismiss the warning and open
System Settings > Privacy & Security. If Open Anyway is available
for Thread Beta, use it and confirm the launch yourself.
Do not disable Gatekeeper or run security-removal commands.
If macOS reports malware or corruption, stop and contact support.

Click the floating pet or press Option-Command-Space to talk.
Closing Settings keeps the pet running. Click the pet to open its voice bubble.
Use the menu bar paw icon to pause following, hide the pet or quit.

Sign in or create your account with email.
Follow the six-step setup. In Connection, choose My Codex subscription to use the official
Codex CLI signed into ChatGPT, or connect an OpenAI/Anthropic key.
Your Codex plan limits or provider API charges apply. API keys are
encrypted in Supabase Vault; Codex credentials stay with Codex.

Start the microphone in the pet bubble. Pause to send your turn;
your buddy replies out loud, then listens again. Tap stop to finish.
On-device recognition requires a supported language. Optional Apple
speech-service fallback is off by default. Voice needs microphone
and speech permission. Share screen captures one previewed JPEG
for your next question only, with Screen Recording permission.
Arrows/highlights guide you. Click, scroll, window and text changes
require individual approval and Accessibility. Screen actions require
a recent screenshot and unchanged target. Some apps limit actions.

Free: 20 requests per UTC day. Pro: A$10/month, no daily Thread quota.
Provider charges, size limits and anti-abuse rate limits still apply.
Sessions live in memory until you quit. No automatic updates yet.
